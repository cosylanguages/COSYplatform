💡 Key English Pronunciation Principles

💡 Essential Rules of English Phonetics

  • 1. The Magic E: A silent E at the end makes the preceding vowel say its name (hat ➔ hate, bit ➔ bite).
  • 2. Soft C and G: C and G sound soft ([s] and [dʒ]) when followed by E, I, or Y (city, gem, gym).
  • 3. The Schwa [ə]: Unstressed vowels in English collapse into the neutral schwa sound (about, banana, doctor).
  • 4. Past Tense -ED: Pronounced /t/ after voiceless sounds (walked), /d/ after voiced sounds (played), and /ɪd/ after T/D (wanted).
