Pedagogical Strategies, Lesson Planning & COSY Ecosystem Integration

📘 How to Use COSYplatform with Students

COSYplatform is structured as a private, direct-link learning management system. Contracted teachers can share specific course or lesson URLs directly with students during or prior to live sessions.

  • Flipped Classroom Model: Send unit pages as pre-lesson readings for students to review vocabulary and grammar rules on COSYtools before live sessions.
  • Structured Live Lessons: Utilize speaking prompts, CELTA CCQs, and speech scripts during live calls or classroom sessions.
  • Asynchronous Homework: Assign interactive lesson tasks and COSYgames practice links to build student consistency.

📝 Lesson Plan Architecture

Recommended 60-Minute Lesson Architecture for COSYplatform Units:

1. Lead-in & Warm-Up (10 mins): Review homework, discuss previous unit's target vocabulary.

2. Core Grammar & Elicitation (15 mins): Introduce new concepts using CELTA CCQs and COSYtools interactive reference tables.

3. Controlled Practice (15 mins): Interactive gap-fills, test questions, or COSYgames multiplayer mode.

4. Freer Practice & Spoken Application (15 mins): Roleplays, debates, and contextual tasks on COSYevents.

5. Wrap-Up & Homework (5 mins): Review unit checklist and set next homework task.

🎮 Game-Based Learning Strategies

Integrating COSYgames increases engagement and retention:

  • Classroom Warm-up Competitions: Start live lessons with a 3-minute timed vocabulary sprint.
  • Team Debates: Use COSYgames prompt cards for instant impromptu debate rounds.

📊 Assessment Rubrics

Evaluate oral and written performance according to CEFR standards:

Criterion Needs Work (1-2) Proficient (3-4) Mastery (5)
Fluency & Flow Frequent hesitation & pauses. Smooth speech with minor pauses. Natural cadence and transition use.
Grammar Precision Frequent structural errors. Minor tense errors, high clarity. Accurate complex tense usage.
Vocabulary Variety Repetitive, simple words. Good range of unit key terms. Rich, nuanced idiomatic terms.

🛠️ Integration with COSYtools

Leverage COSYtools during live explanations:

  • Use the interactive verb conjugator to demonstrate tense modifications in real-time.
  • Direct students to audio pronunciation models for difficult vocabulary phonetics.
  • Utilize the grammar syntax analyzer for instant correction of written assignments.
