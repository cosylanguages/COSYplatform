# A0–A1 inspired eight-lesson review units

This is a condensed eight-lesson review track built from the eight-module arc in the course inspiration: First Contact, Everyday Spaces, Daily Rhythm, People and Likes, Out and About, Food Money and Choices, Looking Back, and Next Steps.

It is integrated into the main General English A0–A1 course as two required review units:

| Course | Unit | Lessons |
|---|---|---|
| A0 | Unit 9. Review & Consolidation | `inspired-01` to `inspired-04` |
| A1 | Unit 13. Review & Consolidation | `inspired-05` to `inspired-08` |

The same eight lessons also work as a standalone intensive track. The standalone roadmap is `roadmaps/general-en-a0-a1-inspired.json`.

Each lesson uses the same 10-slide structure as the main course: communication goal, recycling, vocabulary and chunks, pronunciation, grammar and controlled practice, reading and listening, speaking and writing, 60/90/120 flows, delivery adaptations, and homework.

## Lesson sequence

| Lesson | File | Theme | Core grammar | Speaking outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `lessons/general-english-a0-a1-inspired/inspired-01-first-contact.json` | Names, greetings, cities, jobs, digital basics | `be`, subject pronouns, possessives, `a/an` with jobs | Introduce yourself and others, spell contact details, talk about jobs |
| 2 | `lessons/general-english-a0-a1-inspired/inspired-02-everyday-spaces.json` | Apartments, cafes, offices, objects, requests | `this/that/these/those`, articles, `there is/are`, `can` requests | Describe a room or workspace and ask for everyday items |
| 3 | `lessons/general-english-a0-a1-inspired/inspired-03-daily-rhythm.json` | Workdays, hybrid routines, time, days | Present simple, telling time, `at/on/in`, frequency | Compare routines and schedule a meeting or coffee |
| 4 | `lessons/general-english-a0-a1-inspired/inspired-04-people-and-likes.json` | Friends, coworkers, preferences, free time | Present simple negatives and questions, object pronouns, `like/love/hate + -ing` | Interview a partner about habits, music, food, and weekends |
| 5 | `lessons/general-english-a0-a1-inspired/inspired-05-out-and-about.json` | Neighbourhoods, transit, directions, plans | Imperatives, prepositions of place, `can/can't`, `let's` | Navigate a city map, suggest plans, and confirm availability |
| 6 | `lessons/general-english-a0-a1-inspired/inspired-06-food-money-choices.json` | Groceries, restaurants, prices, comparison | Countable and uncountable nouns, `some/any`, `how much/many`, `would like`, `one/ones`, `too + adjective` | Order food, check prices, and plan a shared meal |
| 7 | `lessons/general-english-a0-a1-inspired/inspired-07-looking-back.json` | Recent weekends, trips, milestones, stories | Past simple `be`, regular and irregular verbs, past questions | Share a short story about a recent weekend, trip, or project |
| 8 | `lessons/general-english-a0-a1-inspired/inspired-08-next-steps.json` | Goals, trips, future plans, review | `going to`, `want/need/would like to`, sequencing | Pitch a short-term goal and outline next steps |

## Teacher editions

The student-safe lessons are in `lessons/general-english-a0-a1-inspired/`. Full teacher editions with notes and answer keys are in `local_content/teacher-lessons/general-english-a0-a1-inspired/`. The local folder is git-ignored, in line with the protected-content contract.

## How to use the review units

- In the main course, use A0 Unit 9 after Unit 8 and A1 Unit 13 after Unit 12. They recycle the language of the preceding units and give learners a strong speaking win before the progress test.
- As a standalone track, load `roadmaps/general-en-a0-a1-inspired.json` for a short intensive course, a summer programme, or a diagnostic track for adults who need a fast A0–A1 overview.
- Use the 40-lesson core course when you need more recycling, more micro-lessons, and more assessment points.
- Both tracks use the same 10-slide lesson format, so teachers can move between them without learning a new structure.
