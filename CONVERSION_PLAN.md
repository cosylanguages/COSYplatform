# COSYevents Session Conversion & Roadmap Mapping Plan

This document inventories all session HTML files from the public repository [`cosylanguages/COSYevents`](https://github.com/cosylanguages/COSYevents) and maps them to `cosylanguages/COSYlanguages` curriculum roadmaps.

## Executive Summary & Track Recommendations

- **Total Sessions Inventoried:** 618 session HTML files across English, French, and Russian clubs.
- **French & Russian Sessions:** Flagged as needing new language roadmaps (`spoken-fr-<level>.json` and `spoken-ru-<level>.json`). No `spoken-fr-*` or `spoken-ru-*` roadmaps currently exist in this repository.
- **Cinema Club Track Recommendation:** Cinema Club sessions (113 sessions) feature a specialized film-based structure (`Screen Hook`, `Scene Study & Adaptation`, `Final Challenge: Director`). **Recommendation:** Dedicated `cinema-english-<level>.json` track instead of merging directly into General Spoken English roadmaps.
