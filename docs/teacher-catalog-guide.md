# CosyLanguages Teacher Catalog & Lesson Guide

## Overview
This guide explains how lessons, curriculums, and teaching manuals are structured in the **CosyLanguages** catalog and platform interface for teachers.

---

## 1. Catalog Hierarchy & Lesson Types
1. **Course Level**: (e.g., *General English A0-C1*, *Spoken English A1-C1*, *Business English*)
2. **Module / Unit**: Specific topical group (e.g., *Unit 1: Lifestyle & Relaxation*, *Unit 2: Work-Life Balance*)
3. **Lesson Types**:
   - **Standard Lessons**: Flexible 50m, 80m, or 110m active teaching formats structured into **16 Live Cards** and **10 Homework Cards**.
   - **Introductory "Aloha" Lessons**: First lesson designed for greeting the student, teacher self-introduction, getting to know the student through games, setting lesson goals, discussing learning styles, presenting the course map, and summarizing lesson results.
   - **Progress Check / Exam**: Assessment slides scheduled after key modules.
   - **Talks / Speaking Clubs**: Short conversational sessions with native speakers or peers.

---

## 2. Monolingual Policy & Concept Check Questions (CCQs)

CosyLanguages operates on a **strictly monolingual English policy**.
- **No L1 Translation**: Never use direct translation exercises or native language explanations.
- **Concept Check Questions (CCQs)**: Verify student comprehension through target questions:
  - *Example for "gastro-tourism"*: "Is gastro-tourism about visiting museums or trying local foods?" (Trying local foods)
  - *Example for "prone to"*: "If you are prone to making mistakes, happens it often or rarely?" (Often)
- **Maximising Student Talk Time (STT)**: Keep teacher explanations brief and prompt students to produce target language in speaking activities.

## 3. Multi-Duration Lesson Timing Models

Lessons scale cleanly across three standard scheduled durations:

### A. 60-Minute Lesson Model (50 Active Teaching Minutes)
| Stage | Active Minutes | Primary Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up & Goal Alignment** | 3–5 min | Review homework, state lesson objectives |
| **Lead-in & Language Input** | 7–10 min | Engage student interest, introduce key lexis/grammar |
| **Controlled Practice** | 10–12 min | Gap fills, multiple choice, drag-and-drop exercises |
| **Freer Practice & Speaking** | 10–12 min | Speech prompts, pair work/discussion, role-play |
| **Cool-down & Feedback** | 5 min | Lesson summary, homework assignment, reflection |

### B. 90-Minute Lesson Model (80 Active Teaching Minutes)
| Stage | Active Minutes | Primary Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up & Homework Review** | 8–10 min | Review previous homework and warm-up speaking |
| **Core Vocab & Grammar Input** | 15–20 min | Deep discovery of language patterns and collocations |
| **Listening / Reading Deep Dive** | 15–20 min | Authentic media analysis, transcript work, gap fills |
| **Freer Practice & Debate/Roleplay**| 20–22 min | Extended group debate, pair discussion, case study |
| **Cool-down & Detailed Feedback** | 7–8 min | Error correction, homework assignment, reflection |

### C. 120-Minute Lesson Model (110 Active Teaching Minutes)
| Stage | Active Minutes | Primary Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up & Homework Discussion** | 10–12 min | Homework review, goal check, warm-up speech |
| **Advanced Language Input & Vocab** | 25–30 min | Extensive vocabulary, idiom, and grammar analysis |
| **Interactive Practice & Media** | 25–30 min | Video/audio tasks, group pair work, matching |
| **Writing Workshop & Debate** | 30–32 min | Live essay drafting, group presentations, role-play |
| **Comprehensive Review & Action Plan**| 8–10 min | Error analysis, action items, homework assignment |

## 4. Teacher Page vs. Student Page Interface Features

### Teacher Page (Live Classroom View)
1. **Stage Aim Box (`<cosy-teacher-notes type="instruction">`)**:
   - Displays pedagogical outcomes and timing targets.
2. **Teacher Speech Scripts & CCQs (`<cosy-teacher-notes type="speech">`)**:
   - Suggested prompts and CCQs to keep lesson flow natural and engaging without L1 translation.
3. **Group Classroom Controls**:
   - Assign breakout rooms, toggle pair-work prompts, control answer overlays, and whiteboard sharing.
4. **Additional / Extension Tasks (`<cosy-teacher-notes type="additional">`)**:
   - Optional extension exercises for 80m/110m formats or quick learners.
5. **Transcripts & Answer Keys (`<cosy-teacher-notes type="tapescript">`)**:
   - Instant access to audio/video scripts and verified answer keys.

### Student Page (Learner Workspace)
- Distraction-free clean slide view.
- Real-time exercise inputs (gap fills, drag-and-drop, dropdowns).
- Direct word addition to personal mobile app vocabulary dictionary.
- Audio voice recorder and writing submission widgets.

---

## 5. Checklist for First / Introductory Lessons

- [ ] **Check limited lesson balance**: Remind student about attendance and milestone exams.
- [ ] **Select lesson duration**: Confirm standard format (60m, 90m, or 120m).
- [ ] **Use introductory slides**: Set goals, discuss interests (e.g. Movies, Tech, Business), and connect to Course Map.
- [ ] **Determine format**: Confirm 1-on-1 vs. Group setting and set expectations.
- [ ] **Establish routine**: Introduce homework tab, Vocabulary trainer, AI practice, and Speaking Clubs.
