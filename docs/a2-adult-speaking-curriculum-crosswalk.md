# A2 Adult Speaking Curriculum Crosswalk

## Executive summary

This document checks whether the attached 8-lesson / 32-unit **CEFR A2 General English for Adults** curriculum can be inserted into the existing COSYplatform A2 General English course.

**Answer: yes, as a speaking-led enrichment overlay — not as a replacement course.**

The existing course already covers the attached grammar scope and more:

- 82 authored teaching lessons across 15 units: 67 core lessons (including 7 gap lessons) and 15 speaking labs.
- 32 A2 grammar topics grounded in COSYmanuals.
- 2,929-word A2 vocabulary slice from COSYdata.
- Reading, listening, speaking, and writing on every lesson.
- 3–6 teacher notes per slide, including at least two discussion prompts.
- 11 assessment lessons built on the A2 speaking rubric and checkpoints.

The attached curriculum adds value in four places:

1. **Adult speaking frames** for each grammar point.
2. **Contemporary adult scenarios** (hybrid work, digital habits, city services, energy and burnout, everyday dilemmas).
3. **A monolingual speaking assessment system** (rubric, mid-course and end-of-course checkpoints, placement/self-assessment).
4. **A four-skill task blueprint** (gist/detail listening, scanning/noticing reading, role-play cards, writing prompt plus peer check).

Those elements should be layered onto the existing lessons. Replacing the 60-lesson roadmap with the 8-lesson outline would lose grammar coverage (past perfect, second conditional, passive, may/might, as...as, do vs make, pronoun review) and break the existing lesson IDs.

## Gap list

These are the only places where the attached curriculum asks for something the existing course does not already teach well enough to enrich in place:

1. **Directions and public transport** (attached 2.2): prepositions of movement, imperatives, platform/connection vocabulary.
2. **Question tags and short reactions** (attached 4.3): `didn't you?`, `was it?`, `So did I`, `Neither did I`.
3. **Polite service requests** (attached 2.3): receipts, returns, appointments, reservations, out of stock.
4. **Digital habits and screen time** (attached 1.1): check notifications, scroll feeds, set boundaries, screen time.
5. **Hybrid and remote work** (attached 1.2): remote work, hybrid schedule, flexible hours, workload negotiation.
6. **Food, cooking and diet** (attached 5.1): ingredients, portions, plant-based, skip meals, grocery list.
7. **Infinitive of purpose** (attached 7.3): `to + verb`, `for + -ing`, `so that`.
8. **Speaking labs** (attached X.4): no dedicated end-of-unit lab lesson in the current 4-lesson units.
9. **Assessment system** (attached rubric and checkpoints): the 11 progress tests, midterm, and final exam are still `planned`.

## Integration plan (completed)

### Keep

- Keep the 15-unit structure and all existing lesson IDs.
- Keep the COSYmanuals grammar IDs and COSYdata vocabulary grounding.
- Keep the existing four-skill slide structure.

### Three phases (completed)

**Phase 1 — Speaking overlay on existing lessons (no new lesson IDs)**

For each existing lesson, add three short teacher-facing elements:

- 2–3 adult speaking frames tied to the lesson grammar.
- One contemporary adult scenario or role-play card.
- One writing prompt with a peer-check step.

This is the lowest-risk change. It preserves the roadmap and makes every lesson match the attached speaking-led model.

**Phase 2 — Seven gap lessons**

Add one lesson per gap, placed at the end of the most relevant unit:

| New lesson | Unit | Grammar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Getting from A to B | Unit 10. City | Prepositions of movement; imperatives |
| Service desk & polite requests | Unit 11. Shopping | Can/Could/Would it be possible |
| Digital habits & screen time | Unit 1. First impression | Present Simple + frequency adverbs |
| Hybrid work & flexible schedules | Unit 5. Get to work! | have to / don't have to |
| Food, cooking & diet | Unit 11. Shopping | Countable/uncountable; quantifiers |
| Question tags & reactions | Unit 3. That's the story! | Question tags; short responses |
| Tools, apps & purpose | Unit 12. Things | Infinitive of purpose; for + -ing |

**Phase 3 — Speaking labs and assessment**

- Add one **Speaking Lab** lesson per unit (15 labs), using the attached Unit X.4 model: ranking, debate, role-play, showcase.
- Build the 11 planned assessment entries with the attached A2 speaking rubric, mid-course checkpoint, and end-of-course checkpoint.
- Add a student self-assessment checklist (see `docs/a2-adult-speaking-assessment.md`).

### Do not

- Do not replace the 60-lesson course with the 8-lesson outline. The attached outline is a subset of the existing grammar scope.
- Do not duplicate the same grammar in a second parallel course. That would create two competing A2 courses.
- Do not rename existing lesson IDs or move lessons between units. The roadmap, classroom catalog, and progress tests depend on them.
