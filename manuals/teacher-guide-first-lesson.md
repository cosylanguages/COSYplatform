# Teacher Guide: First Lesson ("Aloha" & Introductory Warm-up)

## Objectives of the First Lesson
1. **Welcome & Icebreaking**: Greet the student and create a comfortable, low-stress environment.
2. **Assess Goals & Needs**: Understand why the student is learning English (career, travel, relocation, personal growth).
3. **Explore Student Interests**: Choose topics (Movies, Tech, Travel, Psychology, Pets, Business) to customize future lesson warm-ups.
4. **Present Course Map**: Walk through the 40-lesson roadmap, milestone tests, and extra platform tools (Vocabulary Trainer, AI Practice, Speaking Clubs, Talks).
5. **Start Main Topic**: Transition into Lesson 1 (*Relaxation and Hygge*).

---

## Lesson Plan Timing (50 Minutes Total)

- **00:00 - 00:20**: Introductory Aloha Part (Icebreaker questions, goals, interests, course map presentation).
- **00:20 - 00:43**: Main Lesson Content (*Relaxation techniques, vocabulary, grammar, speaking practice*).
- **00:43 - 00:50**: Cool-down & Homework assignment (Skyeng/CosyLanguages mobile app, vocabulary review).

---

## Key Rules & Guidelines

- **Rescheduling vs. Cancellation**: Encourage students to reschedule rather than cancel to maintain steady progress toward the final exam.
- **Active Note Taking**: Take notes during the Aloha section regarding student interests and target dates—you will use these to select tailored extra exercises and warm-ups.
- **Cool-down**: Always preserve 5–7 minutes at the end of class for student feedback and homework instructions.
